## Amazon description

On remote Ashmore Island, people disappear without bodies, without evidence and without explanation.

Aana Voss has spent four years documenting the pattern since her younger brother, Nils, vanished after clocking into a night shift at Halverson's Departmental Store. The store's system recorded him arriving. It never recorded him leaving.

He is not the only one.

As Aana pushes deeper into the island's history, the mystery expands beyond a missing-person case. Beneath the ordinary routines of two isolated towns lies a hidden infrastructure, an ancient biological entity and a bloodline that may explain why the island has been waiting.

The Store at the Edge of Everything is a slow-burn atmospheric horror mystery about grief, evidence, memory and the terrifying possibility that the pattern is deliberate.

Season One: The Blood Remembers.

## Category strategy

KDP currently allows three categories. Because category trees vary by marketplace and format, select the closest available placements in this order of intent:

1. Fiction / Horror — closest accurate general or atmospheric placement available.
2. Fiction / Thrillers / Psychological — or the closest psychological-suspense placement available.
3. Fiction / Thrillers / Suspense — use this before a science-fiction category unless the live KDP taxonomy presents a clearly accurate biological/speculative subcategory.

Do not force the book into a misleading low-competition category. Use Amazon.com as the primary category-selection marketplace first, then mirror the closest relevant options for other marketplaces/formats.
