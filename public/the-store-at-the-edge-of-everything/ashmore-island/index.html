The Store at the Edge of Everything / Ashmore Island

Ashmore Island

Ashmore Island is the fictional remote-island setting at the centre of The Store at the Edge of Everything, Season One: The Blood Remembers.

Where is Ashmore Island?

Within the story, Ashmore Island lies in the Norwegian Sea. It is isolated from the mainland and divided between two small communities: Keld on the western shore and Morven on the eastern side. One road connects the island. At the exact middle of that road stands Halverson's Departmental Store.

Ashmore Island is fictional. Its geography was created for the novel's closed, investigative atmosphere: a place where distance, weather, limited transport and a small population make every disappearance harder to dismiss and every pattern harder to escape.

Keld and Morven

Keld

The smaller western town, with a population of roughly four hundred in the story. Keld depends heavily on Halverson's for supplies, and its shrinking population reinforces the feeling of a community that has learned to live around absence.

Morven

The larger eastern town, with roughly eleven hundred residents. It is louder and more self-sufficient than Keld, but it remains part of the same island system and the same long-running pattern.

Why the island matters

Ashmore is not only a backdrop. The island's isolation changes how evidence moves, how rumours survive, how institutions behave and how residents adapt to things they cannot explain. People leave; others stay; some disappear. Over time, the island has developed its own habits of silence.

That isolation also lets Aana Voss see something outsiders have repeatedly failed to see: the disappearances are not ordinary missing-person cases. They form a pattern, and Halverson's sits uncomfortably close to its centre.

The island and the store

Everyone on Ashmore knows Halverson's. Everyone needs it. The store is positioned between the island's two towns and functions as ordinary retail by day, yet its architecture, closing rules and history suggest that it was built to do more than sell goods.

The tension of Season One comes from that contradiction: Ashmore Island depends on the same place that may be connected to what the island fears most.
