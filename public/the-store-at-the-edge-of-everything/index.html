Official synopsis

One island. Two towns. One store.

Aana Voss is twenty-five, an investigator and writer who has spent years tracking the gaps in official explanations. Her younger brother, Nils, disappeared after clocking into a night shift at Halverson's Departmental Store. He never clocked out.

He is not the only one. Ashmore Island has accumulated a pattern of disappearances that ordinary explanations can no longer contain. As Aana pushes deeper, the mystery expands beyond a missing-person case into a hidden infrastructure, an ancient biological entity and a bloodline that may be the reason the island has been waiting.

The horror is slow-burn and atmospheric: less about jump scares than rules, evidence, memory, isolation and the terrifying possibility that the pattern is deliberate.

The world

Ashmore Island

A fictional remote island in the Norwegian Sea. Keld lies on the western shore, Morven on the east. Between them sits Halverson's Departmental Store: necessary to island life, inexplicably well supplied and connected to a history nobody wants to state plainly.

Questions & answers

About the book

What is The Store at the Edge of Everything about?

It follows Aana Voss as she investigates disappearances linked to Halverson's Departmental Store on Ashmore Island, including the disappearance of her brother. The investigation reveals a deeper biological mystery involving the island, the store and a specific bloodline.

Who wrote The Store at the Edge of Everything?

It was written by Shreyansh Singh.

What genre is the book?

It blends atmospheric horror, mystery thriller, psychological suspense and biological horror.

Is Ashmore Island real?

No. Ashmore Island is a fictional setting created for the story.

Is it supernatural horror?

The story uses the atmosphere and mythology of supernatural horror, but its central entity is framed biologically rather than as a conventional ghost, vampire or supernatural creature.

Is it part of a larger story?

Yes. This publication is Season One, subtitled The Blood Remembers, within a broader story universe.
